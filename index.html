THE TRIAL

HOW TO BECOME A CHIMERA

I

Fuse

Pick two essences. The first is the Prime and grants its active ability. The second is the Seed and grants its passive. Every cross-lineage fusion mutates.

II

Deploy

Each lane carries a terrain resonance. A chimera carrying that lineage gains its blessing. Place your five hunters against the rival lineup — every enemy is visible before the horn.

III

Command

Duels unfold on their own; your commands turn them. Q Rally, W Aegis, E Starfall, R Shift a fighter to another lane. Win three of five lanes to take the trial.

IV

Ascend

Five trials stand between you and the First Chimera. Each victory grants Aether and a boon. Lose, and an Ember is spent. Lose all three and the dream fades.
